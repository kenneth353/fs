  • Destination

The passenger negotiates the fare with the rider. The fare is discussed based on:
  • Distance
  • Route
  • Drop-off location
  • Road conditions
  • Travel time

17. Panel Explanation Guide

Simple scripts you can use when presenting each major workflow.

How do I explain the Passenger side?
"The passenger creates a ride request by providing the pickup and destination. The passenger and rider negotiate the fare. Once they agree, the fare is locked and the system generates a DRM-QR. The rider scans the QR during pickup before the trip can start. GPS tracking then monitors the ride, and after reaching the destination, the passenger pays the agreed fare."
How do I explain the Rider side?
"The rider receives the dispatched request, accepts within the 10–15 second acceptance period, travels to the pickup point, scans the passenger's DRM-QR, and after successful authentication starts the trip. GPS tracking remains active during the ride, and the rider collects the agreed fare."
How do I explain the Admin/Operations side?
"Operations monitors the system and documented operational issues during the ride process, responds when support is needed, and reviews booking, transaction, and trip records during end-of-day operations."
Why is DRM-QR important?
"It authenticates the passenger and ride before the trip starts and helps prevent pickup errors and off-platform rides."
How do I explain the Voyago System?
"The system processes the booking, handles the fare negotiation state, locks the agreed fare, generates and verifies the DRM-QR, dispatches the ride, monitors the active ride via GPS, and records the transaction."
