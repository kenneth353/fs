REQUESTED

Passenger submitted a ride request.

SEARCHING

System is looking for an available rider.

ASSIGNED

A rider has accepted the ride request.

ARRIVING

Rider is on the way to the pickup location.

ARRIVED

Rider has arrived at the pickup point.

IN PROGRESS

Ride is currently ongoing.

COMPLETED

Ride has been successfully finished.

CANCELLED

Ride was cancelled by passenger or rider.

NO RIDER FOUND [FC]

No available rider accepted the request.
