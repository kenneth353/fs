PASSENGER Step 01 — Open / Access Voyago
The passenger opens the Voyago mobile application, or contacts Voyago through Messenger or SMS as a backup booking method.
PASSENGER Step 02 — Create Ride Request
The passenger provides their pickup location and destination. The system uses this information to prepare the ride request.
PASSENGER Step 03 — Fare Negotiation
The passenger participates in an in-platform fare negotiation with the rider. The fare is discussed based on:
  • Distance
  • Route
  • Travel time
  • Road conditions
How to explain this to the panel:
"The fare is not simply assumed by the system. The passenger and rider agree on the trip fare based on the trip conditions. Once they agree, the fare is locked before the trip begins."
PASSENGER Step 04 — Fare Agreement
Fare Proposed
Discussion / Negotiation
Agreement

SYSTEM Step 05 — DRM-QR Generation
After the ride is confirmed and the fare is locked, the Voyago system generates the passenger's Digital Ride-Match QR (DRM-QR) code.
RIDER Step 06 — Rider Dispatch
The selected rider receives the trip details, including pickup location, destination, and the agreed fare.
RIDER Step 07 — Pickup
The rider travels to the passenger's pickup location. The passenger can track the rider and estimated arrival time through the app.
SYSTEM Step 08 — QR Verification
The passenger presents the DRM-QR. The rider scans it using the driver app. The system verifies that the QR matches the passenger and ride details.
SYSTEM Step 09 — Trip Start
Only after successful QR verification does the trip officially start. The "Start Trip" feature remains locked until verification is complete.
SYSTEM Step 10 — Trip In Progress
GPS tracking is active during the trip. The operations team can monitor the active ride.
RIDER Step 11 — Destination
The rider transports the passenger to the agreed destination.
SYSTEM Step 12 — Payment
The passenger pays the agreed fare through cash or available digital payment options. Voyago records its booking fee, while the rider keeps the agreed trip fare.
SYSTEM Step 13 — Completion
The system records the completed transaction. All trip and payment information is logged digitally.

19. Panel Explanation Guide

Simple scripts you can use when presenting each major workflow.

Booking & Fare Negotiation:
"First, the passenger creates a ride request by entering the pickup and destination. The passenger and rider then agree on the fare based on the trip conditions — distance, route, travel time, and road conditions. Once the fare is confirmed, the system generates the DRM-QR and dispatches the request to the rider."
Ride Dispatching:
"The Voyago Dispatch System sends the ride request to a selected rider. The rider has a 10 to 15-second acceptance window. If the rider accepts, they travel to the pickup location. If not, the request is handled by the dispatch system."
DRM-QR Verification:
"At pickup, the rider scans the passenger's DRM-QR. The system verifies whether the QR matches the passenger and ride details. If verification is successful, the trip can officially start. If not, the trip cannot proceed and support is alerted."
Trip Execution & GPS:
"Once verified, the trip starts and GPS tracking becomes active. The operations team can monitor the active ride. An automatic trip time-out feature detects delayed pickups or unusual route changes during late-night trips."
Payment & Recording:
"After reaching the destination, the passenger pays the agreed fare through cash or available digital payment. Voyago records its booking fee, while the rider keeps the agreed trip fare. All transactions are digitally recorded."
End-of-Day Operations:
"After each shift, drivers submit their trip records and undergo vehicle safety checks. The operations team reviews transactions, booking records, and prepares reports for proper service and financial monitoring."
