PASSENGER Step 1 — Book a Ride
The passenger initiates a ride request through the Voyago app, or contacts through Messenger/SMS as a backup booking method.
PASSENGER Step 2 — Enter Pickup and Destination
The passenger provides:
  • Pickup location
  • Destination
PASSENGER Step 3 — Fare Negotiation
The passenger negotiates the fare with the rider. The fare is discussed based on:
  • Distance
  • Route
  • Drop-off location
  • Road conditions
  • Travel time
How to explain this to the panel:
"The fare is not assumed by the system. The passenger and rider agree on the trip fare based on the trip conditions. Once they agree, the fare is locked before the trip begins."
PASSENGER Step 4 — Fare Agreement
Once both parties agree, the fare becomes fixed/locked for the trip.

SYSTEM Step 5 — DRM-QR Generation
After the fare is agreed and confirmed, the passenger receives the unique Digital Ride-Match QR (DRM-QR) associated with the ride.
SYSTEM Step 6 — Ride Dispatching
The ride request is dispatched to a rider according to the Voyago operational process.
RIDER Step 7 — Rider Goes to Pickup
The rider travels to the passenger's pickup location. The passenger waits for the rider.
System Response: Passenger can track the rider through the app.
PASSENGER Step 8 — DRM-QR Verification
The passenger presents the DRM-QR and the rider scans it using the rider-side system.
System Response: The system verifies the ride information.
SYSTEM Step 9 — Authentication
Authentication Successful?
NO
Ride Denied / Support
YES
Trip Verified — Trip Can Start
SYSTEM Step 10 — Trip Execution
After successful authentication, the trip starts. GPS tracking is active during the trip.
System Response: Operations can monitor the active ride through GPS.
RIDER Step 11 — Destination
The rider brings the passenger to the agreed destination.
PASSENGER Step 12 — Payment
The passenger pays the agreed fare through the available payment method (cash or available digital payment options).
System Response: Voyago records its booking fee, while the rider keeps the agreed trip fare. All transactions are digitally recorded.
SYSTEM Step 13 — Completion
The trip is completed and the transaction is recorded.

How to explain this to the panel:
"After the passenger confirms the ride, the system locks the agreed fare, generates the DRM-QR, and dispatches the request to a rider. The rider accepts within 10–15 seconds, travels to the pickup location, and scans the passenger's DRM-QR. The system verifies the QR, and if successful, the trip starts. GPS monitoring remains active until the destination is reached, after which payment is collected and the transaction is recorded."

17. Panel Explanation Guide

Simple scripts you can use when presenting each major workflow.

What is the main flow?
"Voyago starts when a passenger books a ride, provides the pickup and destination, negotiates the fare, and confirms the agreed fare."
Why is the fare locked?
"Once the passenger and rider agree on the fare, the agreed amount is locked before the trip starts."
What is DRM-QR?
"DRM-QR is the ride authentication mechanism used to verify the passenger's booking before the trip starts."
What happens at pickup?
"The rider scans the passenger's DRM-QR. If authentication is successful, the trip can start."
What does the system monitor?
"The system supports GPS tracking and ride monitoring, including automatic trip timeout detection for delayed pickups or unusual route changes."
What does the Admin do?
"Operations monitors rides, responds to operational issues, reviews records, and handles end-of-day operations."
How does the ride end?
"After reaching the destination, the passenger pays the agreed fare, the trip is completed, and the transaction is recorded."
